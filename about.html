About NAC

Nutrition Assessment Calculator

Nutrition assessment involves a lot of repeated arithmetic: energy equations, body weight references, protein and fluid targets, and screening scores. Doing these by hand is slow and easy to get wrong. NAC puts the most commonly used calculations in one place, so you choose a calculation, enter the values, and get the result along with the source it comes from.

Who made it

NAC is made by %VITE_OWNER_NAME%, a Human Nutrition and Dietetics student in Pakistan, for fellow students and practising dietitians and nutritionists. It is free to use.

What's included

  • Energy: Mifflin-St Jeor and Harris-Benedict (1984 revision), with activity factors
  • Body composition: BMI with WHO and Asia-Pacific cut-offs, and Ideal Body Weight (Devine)
  • Screening: percentage weight loss with Blackburn severity grading, and the MUST score
  • Macronutrients: protein requirements for clinical conditions and training goals
  • Fluids: the adult 30–35 mL/kg estimate and the Holliday-Segar method for children

How results are checked

Each calculator lists the published papers or guidelines it is based on, directly underneath it, and each protein preset names its guideline in the note beside it. Where a value is a general clinical estimate rather than a published equation, the text says so. The formulas have been checked by hand against worked examples. NAC is a support tool: please read the health disclaimer.

Privacy

Values you enter stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. There are no accounts and no analytics. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Accessibility

NAC aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA. It can be used fully with a keyboard, is built to work with screen readers, supports light and dark themes, and uses colour combinations checked for contrast in both. If something is hard to use, please let me know.
