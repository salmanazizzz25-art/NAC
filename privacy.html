In short: NAC does not ask for your name, does not have accounts, and does not send the values you type into the calculators anywhere. Calculations happen on your own device. If advertising is shown, Google may use cookies as described below.

Who runs NAC

NAC (Nutrition Assessment Calculator) is a free tool made and run by %VITE_OWNER_NAME%. You can contact me at %VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL%.

Information you enter

Everything you type into a calculator (weight, height, age and so on) stays in your browser. It is used only to show you the result, and it is not sent to me, stored on a server, or shared with anyone. When you close or refresh the page, those values are gone.

Please do not treat NAC as a place to keep patient records. It has no way to save them, and you should not enter names or other details that identify a person.

Information stored on your device

  • Theme preference. If you choose light or dark mode with the toggle, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so NAC remembers it next time. It contains only the word "light" or "dark".
  • Offline copy of the app. NAC uses a service worker to keep a copy of its own files on your device so it loads quickly and works offline. This contains the app's code and images, not information about you.

You can remove both at any time by clearing site data for NAC in your browser settings.

Hosting

NAC is hosted by Vercel. Like any website host, Vercel's servers automatically process technical information needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, and may keep it in short-term logs for security and reliability. See Vercel's privacy policy. I do not use these logs to identify visitors.

NAC does not use any analytics or tracking tools of its own.

Advertising and cookies

To help cover running costs, NAC may show ads provided by Google AdSense. NAC places its ad in a box labelled "Advertisement", below the calculators and separate from the results. Google's ad script runs only on the calculator page, not on this page or the About and Terms pages.

When Google's ad script is loaded:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before advertising cookies are used, and can change their choice later from the privacy and cookie settings link that Google's message provides.

Children

NAC is made for dietitians, nutritionists, other health professionals and students. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and deletion requests

Because NAC does not collect or store personal information on a server, there is no account or profile to delete on my side. You can:

  • clear NAC's site data in your browser to remove the theme setting and offline copy;
  • manage or delete Google advertising data through your Google account and the links above;
  • email %VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL% with any privacy question or request. I will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If NAC starts handling information differently, for example if saved history or accounts are added, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
